# ShieldDetermine

### 角色能否创造护盾

---

<details><summary>使用类型</summary>

##### 关于能否创造护盾buff

结晶护盾与命座护盾不计入此项

</details>

---

<details><summary>Buff侧设置</summary>

##### 通用配置
~~~~~~~~~~
params.ShieldDetermine === true ? true : false
~~~~~~~~~~

</details>

---

<details><summary>Dmg侧设置</summary>

此项可留空，留空默认false

</details>
